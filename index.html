The questions managers ask.

We keep everything in a spreadsheet. How does it get in?

Send us what you keep today. Names, courses and dates come across, so nobody retypes them.

What is the contract?

Monthly rolling, priced per home. Cancel any month and take all your data with you.

What does it cost?

Your first home is free for a month. After that it is a monthly price per home. You get the number on the call.

Will Ofsted accept it?

The evidence pack holds the same records you would show from folders, dated and signed, in one download. Staffbook is a way to keep and show your records. It does not change what Ofsted asks for.

Can staff see each other’s records?

No. A support worker sees their own file, their own tasks and the published rota. Everything else needs a manager login.

We run more than one home.

You see every home in one place, and each home on its own. The price is per home.
